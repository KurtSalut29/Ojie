Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

Ojie is a mobile app that helps students track their OJT (on-the-job training). This policy explains what information Ojie collects, why, who can see it, and what you can do about it. Ojie follows the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

Ojie is developed by [DEVELOPER NAME]. For any privacy question or request, email [CONTACT EMAIL].

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and profile photo from your Google account when you sign in, and whether you are a student or an OJT coordinator.
  • OJT profile: your school, program, required hours, start date, work schedule, host company and supervisor's name. You can skip any of these.
  • Time records: your time in, time out, breaks, absences, holidays and any edits you make to them.
  • Journal: your daily check-in answers, mood stickers, tags and the photos you choose to add.
  • Requirements: your requirement checklist and its status.
  • Emergency contacts you choose to save in the app.
  • Coordinators: the batches you create, the requirements you set, and the school email used to verify your account.
  • App and device data: crash reports, basic usage events (for example, which screens are opened), device model, app version and a notification token so we can send you reminders.

We do not collect your location in the current version of Ojie. If a later version adds an optional location stamp, it will only be taken at time in and time out, only after you turn it on, and this policy will be updated first.

2. Why we use it

  • To compute your OJT hours, progress and projected end date.
  • To keep your records backed up and synced across your devices.
  • To send the reminders and notifications you choose.
  • To connect you to your coordinator's batch, only if you join one.
  • To find and fix crashes and improve the app.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. What your coordinator can see

Joining a coordinator's batch is optional. Before you join, the app shows you exactly what will be shared and asks for your consent. If you join, your coordinator can see only:

  • your hours and time logs (including which entries were edited),
  • your requirement status and the documents you submit,
  • supervisor signatures, and
  • anything you choose to submit to them.
Your coordinator can never see your moods, your journal entries that you have not submitted, your rank, badges or streaks. You can leave a batch at any time.

4. Who stores your data

We use trusted service providers to run Ojie. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and file storage, hosted in Singapore.
  • PowerSync: syncs your data between your phone and our database so the app works offline, hosted in the United States.
  • Google Firebase: push notifications, crash reports and usage analytics.
  • Google Sign-In: to let you sign in with your Google account.

Some of these providers store data outside the Philippines. We choose providers that protect data with encryption in transit and access controls.

5. How we protect it

  • Data is encrypted in transit between your phone and our servers.
  • Access rules in our database make sure each person can only reach the data they are allowed to see.
  • You can lock the app with a PIN or fingerprint (when available).

6. How long we keep it

We keep your records while your account exists, including after your OJT ends, so you can use them later (for example, for job applications). You can delete your account at any time, and we will delete your data as described on our account deletion page.

7. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to be informed, to access your data, to correct it, to object, to have it deleted, and to get a copy of it. You can also file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission. To use any of these rights, email [CONTACT EMAIL].

8. Children

Ojie is meant for college and senior high school students in OJT or work immersion. If you are under 18, please use Ojie with the knowledge of your parent or guardian.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and tell you in the app before the change affects your data.
