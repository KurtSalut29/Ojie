Delete your Ojie account

You can delete your Ojie account and all of its data at any time.

Option 1: In the app

  1. Open Ojie and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and data are deleted.

Before deleting, you can download a copy of your records from the same screen.

Option 2: By email

If you can no longer open the app, email kurtsalut18@gmail.com from the email address of your Ojie account, with the subject "Delete my Ojie account". We will confirm and delete it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and profile
  • Your time records, journal, moods, photos, tags and requirements
  • Your emergency contacts and app settings

What may be kept

Documents you already submitted to your coordinator may stay in your school's records, because they became part of your school's OJT files. Backups are erased on their normal cycle within 30 days.
