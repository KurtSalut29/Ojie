# Ojie Pose Prompts (para sa ChatGPT)

## Paano gamitin

1. Magbukas ng **bagong chat** sa ChatGPT para sa bawat pose, para hindi maghalo ang mga naunang drawing.
2. **I-attach ang `design/mascot/Ojie.png`** sa bawat prompt. Ito ang reference.
3. Kopyahin ang **buong prompt**: ang "Character" block **at** ang prompt ng pose.
4. Kapag maganda ang resulta, i-download ito at i-save sa `design/mascot/poses/` gamit ang **eksaktong filename** na nakasulat.
5. Kapag hindi transparent ang background, sabihin sa ChatGPT: *"Make the background fully transparent, PNG."*
6. Sabihin kay Claude kapag may bago, at ire-review niya ito katabi ng iba.

Huwag tanggapin ang resulta kung **iba ang kulay ng mata, ang bandana, ang guhit, o ang proporsyon** kumpara sa reference. Ulitin na lang.

---

## Character block (isama sa SIMULA ng bawat prompt)

```
Use the attached image as the exact character reference. This is Ojie, the mascot of an app for Filipino OJT (internship) students. Keep the character IDENTICAL to the reference:
- a chubby, chibi black-and-dark-gray tabby kitten with darker tiger stripes on the head, back, arms and tail
- fluffy spiky tufts on top of the head, large rounded ears with cream-pink insides
- big round amber-orange eyes with a white highlight, small black nose, cream-white muzzle
- cream-white chest, paws and tail tip; soft pink paw beans
- an orange bandana tied around the neck, knot on the character's left side, two tails of fabric flowing out
- thick dark outlines, clean cel shading, glossy "sticker" illustration style, exactly the same line weight and colors as the reference
- same body proportions (big head, small body), same size of eyes and ears

Output rules:
- square 1024 x 1024 PNG with a FULLY TRANSPARENT background
- the character centered, full body visible, about 85% of the canvas height, with even empty space around it
- no text, no letters, no logo, no background scenery, no ground shadow, no frame or border
- only ONE character in the image
```

## Checklist bago tanggapin ang isang pose

- [ ] Kamukha ng reference: kulay ng mata, bandana, guhit, at ang cream na dibdib
- [ ] Parehong kapal ng outline at istilo
- [ ] Transparent ang background (walang puti o checkerboard na naka-drawing)
- [ ] Walang text o logo
- [ ] Buong katawan, nakagitna, at may espasyo sa paligid
